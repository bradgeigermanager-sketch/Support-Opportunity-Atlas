Opportunity Atlas v1.0

A searchable map of needs, opportunities, skills, resources, organizations, and impact pathways.

Instead of searching:

"Find me an NGO."

Users search:

"I'm a programmer with 5 hours/week. What educational opportunities need software help in Canada?"

or

"What are the biggest unmet legal education needs in rural communities?"

Future Vision: Opportunity Atlas 2.0

Imagine a system where you enter:

Skills:
Programming
Writing
Teaching

Interests:
Education
Legal Aid

Location:
Canada

Time:
4 Hours/Week


And it instantly produces:

unmet needs matching your skills,
organizations working on those needs,
volunteer and career opportunities,
learning paths,
projects you could start yourself,
estimated impact.

That transforms nonprofit discovery from organization-centered to impact-centered. The question becomes:

"Given who I am and what I can contribute, where can I make the greatest difference?"
