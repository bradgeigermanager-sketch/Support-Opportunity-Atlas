For accessibility, every form control should have:

A visible label (<label>)
A descriptive hint/help text
An accessible association using for, id, and aria-describedby
Improved Page Size Selector
<div class="page-size-control">

    <label for="pageSize">
        Opportunities per page
    </label>

    <p
        id="pageSizeHint"
        class="field-hint"
    >
        Choose how many opportunities appear on each page.
        Your selection is saved for future visits.
    </p>

    <select
        id="pageSize"
        aria-describedby="pageSizeHint"
    >
        <option value="6">6</option>
        <option value="9">9</option>
        <option value="12">12</option>
        <option value="24">24</option>
        <option value="48">48</option>
        <option value="999999">All</option>
    </select>

</div>

Hint Styling
.field-hint{

    margin:4px 0 8px;

    font-size:14px;

    color:#666;

    line-height:1.4;

}

Even Better for Screen Readers

Provide additional instructions that screen readers announce automatically:

<select
    id="pageSize"
    aria-describedby="
        pageSizeHint
        pageSizeSavedHint
    "
>

<p
    id="pageSizeHint"
    class="field-hint"
>
    Choose how many opportunities appear on each page.
</p>

<p
    id="pageSizeSavedHint"
    class="sr-only"
>
    Your choice will be remembered the next time
    you visit Opportunity Atlas.
</p>

.sr-only{

    position:absolute;

    width:1px;

    height:1px;

    padding:0;

    margin:-1px;

    overflow:hidden;

    clip:rect(0,0,0,0);

    white-space:nowrap;

    border:0;

}

Accessibility Pattern for All Filters

Use the same structure:

<label for="cause">
    Cause Area
</label>

<p
    id="causeHint"
    class="field-hint"
>
    Filter opportunities by the problem they help solve.
</p>

<select
    id="cause"
    aria-describedby="causeHint"
>
    ...
</select>

<label for="skill">
    Skill
</label>

<p
    id="skillHint"
    class="field-hint"
>
    Show opportunities matching a specific skill.
</p>

<select
    id="skill"
    aria-describedby="skillHint"
>
    ...
</select>

<label for="search">
    Search Opportunities
</label>

<p
    id="searchHint"
    class="field-hint"
>
    Search by opportunity title, cause, skill, or contribution type.
</p>

<input
    id="search"
    type="text"
    aria-describedby="searchHint"
>


This pattern makes the Opportunity Atlas much more accessible for screen-reader users, keyboard users, and people who benefit from clear instructions.
