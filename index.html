A connected system

More than an organization directory

Start with a problem, a skill, a resource, a place, or an organization. The Atlas connects those views through shared identifiers and typed relationships.

Match capabilities

Compare skills, qualifications, time, resources, location, and preferences with transparent criteria.

04

Measure what changes

Track inputs, activities, outputs, outcomes, and impact without confusing one for another.

Version 2.0 architecture

Built for evidence, reuse, and continuous improvement

OntologyShared entities, controlled terms, and typed relationships.
ProvenanceSource traceability, evidence states, confidence, and review history.
GovernanceSeparate need verification from opportunity availability and publication.
MatchingExplainable compatibility with explicit constraints and unknowns.
ImpactBaselines, indicators, outputs, outcomes, and attribution limits.
InteroperabilityVersioned JSON schemas and a relational-plus-JSONB data model.

Evidence principle: a reported need, a corroborated need, a verified need, and an actively solicited opportunity are different states. The Atlas preserves those distinctions.
