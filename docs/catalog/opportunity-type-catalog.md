# Opportunity Type Catalog

## Purpose

This controlled vocabulary describes the general mechanism or form of an opportunity. It is not a directory of live solicitations. A type term does not establish that an opportunity exists, is legitimate, or is currently available.

A listing may have one primary type and multiple secondary types. Keep opportunity type separate from domain, action, participant relationship, delivery mode, and availability.

## Classification dimensions

- **Opportunity type:** the mechanism (grant, job, volunteer shift, equipment access).
- **Domain:** the subject area (health, education, housing, environment).
- **Action:** what a participant actually does (teach, repair, research, deliver).
- **Relationship:** volunteer, employee, contractor, grantee, donor, learner, partner, member, or public participant.
- **Contribution mode:** time, expertise, money, goods, facilities, data, access, advocacy, or capacity.
- **Delivery mode:** in-person, remote, hybrid, mobile, or place-based.
- **Availability:** open, waitlist, limited capacity, not accepting, or unknown.
- **Maturity:** idea, proposed, seeking partners, accepting interest, accepting applications, active delivery, recurring, or concluded.

## Publication safeguards

- A catalog type is not evidence that a listing exists.
- A search result or old announcement does not prove applications remain open.
- Distinguish open, waitlist, paused, filled, closed, and unknown.
- Record source and date status was checked.
- Never infer compensation, eligibility, safety, legitimacy, or availability from a title.
- State whether participation is paid, unpaid, stipend-based, reimbursed, in-kind, fee-charging, or unknown.
- Verify publisher and contact channel before promoting consequential listings.
- Apply relevant legal, licensing, safeguarding, research ethics, and safety review.
- Preserve source assertions, conflicts, corrections, and review history.
- Allow multiple types and domains rather than forcing a single label.
- Do not expose unnecessary beneficiary or participant personal information.

## Example classification

A hypothetical community repair workshop could use primary type opp.build.repair; secondary types opp.volunteer.skilled and opp.network.facility; domains domain.community_development and domain.infrastructure; actions repair, teach, and provide; contribution modes time, tools, materials, and facility; delivery in-person; relationship volunteer. Its availability remains unknown until checked with the responsible organizer.

## Taxonomy governance

New terms require an ID, label, definition, scope boundaries, examples and non-examples, parent term, synonyms, language labels, source or rationale, version, and status. Do not reuse retired IDs. Prefer adding a narrower child term over silently changing an established definition.
