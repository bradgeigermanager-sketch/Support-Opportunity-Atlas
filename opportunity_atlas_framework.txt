The Ultimate Vision

The Opportunity Atlas is not a directory of NGOs.

It's a map of:

Needs
+
Problems
+
Risks
+
Knowledge Gaps
+
Untapped Opportunities
+
Available Resources
+
Potential Helpers


The central question becomes:

"Given my skills, time, interests, and resources, where can I create the most positive impact right now?"

That question works whether you're a student, teacher, programmer, scientist, lawyer, artist, retiree, entrepreneur, donor, volunteer, or future nonprofit founder.
